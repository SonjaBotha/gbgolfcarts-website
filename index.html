A hands-on business

You deal with a real person who cares about the work.

At GB Golf Carts, we believe good service starts with listening, understanding the problem and finding a sensible solution.

Willie is a practical, hands-on problem solver who takes pride in doing things properly. He doesn't believe in cutting corners just to get a job out the door.

If something can be fixed, improved or restored, he'll look at the options and work out a sensible way forward. If a job falls outside his expertise, he'll tell you honestly and point you in the right direction.

Customers come back because they value reliable work, straightforward answers and personal service — not empty promises.

Our way of doing business

We look for good-quality parts at sensible prices, including sourcing options locally and internationally when needed. Experience and a strong network help us tackle problems that aren't always straightforward.

And when the work is done, we want you to feel comfortable coming back if you need help again.
