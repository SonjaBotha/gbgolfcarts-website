Welcome to GB Golfcarts

A premier family-owned golf cart service provider nestled in the picturesque locale of Gordon's Bay, Western Cape.

After I started playing golf in 2005, familiarising myself with the sport & making use of golf carts on a regular basis, I realised the gap in the market.

The business venture started in 2009 as a hobby. With honest hands-on service to our clients, we have grown stronger and today we are still a hardworking, professional business, competing in a competitive market.

Thanks to our loyal customers we are offered the chance to make a difference by our unique business methods & service rendering.

We strive to serve the market to outdo all competitors by means of service levels, prices and professional advice. It is our mission to serve the Golf cart community with professional and high service levels.

Your all-in-one golf cart solution

GB Golf-carts is a service based company. We Sell, Buy, Service, Refurbish, Spray, Rebuild, Re-wire and customise all types of golf carts and even do cart sitting for travelling owners.

🚚 Pick-up and Delivery

We can pick up and deliver your cart for repairs. Just give us a call and set up an appointment. Drive In's welcome.

🔧 Maintenance & Service

We offer maintenance programs and preventative maintenance vital to keep your cart running. We service all makes.

🎨 Speciality & Custom

From roll bar to complete body, tyres to tops, complete rewiring, upholstery, spray-painting and canopies - we do it all.

💰 Sales & Advice

Looking to purchase good quality new or used? Or sell yours? We sell all spare parts and happy to just give advice.

🏖️ Cart Sitting

While you're on holiday, or chasing the summer around the world, we look after and keep your cart in good running order.

🏭 Beyond Golf

We operate in private and public sectors - security, mining, manufacturing, logistics, agriculture, elderly and disabled mobility.
